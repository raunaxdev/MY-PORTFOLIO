About Me

My Story

I am Raunak Kumar Jha, a BCA student and aspiring full stack developer with a strong interest in web development, programming, databases, and problem-solving. I enjoy building clean, responsive, and useful web applications that combine good design with practical functionality.

My journey in technology started with curiosity and has grown into a consistent learning path. I have worked on practical projects such as a local problem reporting app, college attendance system, animated websites, and modern UI designs. These projects helped me understand real-world development, user experience, responsive design, and database integration.

Along with development, I also create beginner-friendly coding content through Raunex CodeLab, where I share my learning journey and help others understand programming in a simple way.

My goal is to keep improving my frontend and backend development skills, build meaningful projects, gain industry experience, and grow as a full stack developer who can create solutions that are useful, reliable, and easy to use.
